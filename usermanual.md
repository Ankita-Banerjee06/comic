# VLQ Platform: User Manual

Welcome to the **VLQ (Visual Learning Platform)**! This manual will guide you through using the core features of the platform: **AMIVI** (Amit's Visuals) and **AMICO** (Amit's Comics).

---

## 1. Getting Started

### Creating an Account
1. Navigate to the **Sign Up** page.
2. The registration form has been pre-filled with demo credentials (e.g., `Alex Chen`, `alex.chen@student.edu`, and password `12345`).
3. Simply click the **Create Account** button to instantly log in and access the dashboard.

---

## 2. Using AMIVI (Amit's Visuals)
AMIVI is designed to turn lengthy, boring text into quick visual chunks, narrated videos, and interactive quizzes.

### Generating a Visual Lesson
1. Click on **AMIVI** in the left-hand navigation sidebar.
2. In the text box provided, paste any educational text, article, or homework topic (e.g., "Explain how the water cycle works").
3. Click **Generate Visuals**.
4. **Wait for Processing**: The AI will now write a script, generate voiceovers, download custom images, and stitch them into a video. This process runs locally and usually takes about **1.5 to 2.5 minutes**. Do not close the page while the "Analyzing Text..." animation is playing.

### Viewing the Results
Once processing is complete, you will see two sections:
- **Final Video**: A fully narrated `.mp4` video that you can play directly in your browser.
- **Generated Slides**: A carousel of the individual visual slides and the accompanying text, allowing you to review the micro-bits at your own pace.

### Taking a Quiz
1. After generating your visual lesson in AMIVI, click the **Take Quiz on this Topic** button.
2. The AI will instantly generate a 5-question multiple-choice quiz based on the exact text you provided.
3. You will be redirected to the Quiz module to test your knowledge!

---

## 3. Using AMICO (Amit's Comics)
AMICO transforms your school homework and topics into engaging, personalized comic books.

### Generating a Comic Book
1. Click on **AMICO** in the left-hand navigation sidebar.
2. Type in a homework topic or a story concept (e.g., "The discovery of gravity by Isaac Newton").
3. Click **Generate Comic**.
4. The AI will write a 4-panel comic script and generate beautiful illustrations for each panel.

### Reading the Comic
- Once generated, the comic will appear on your screen in a beautiful Comic Reader interface. 
- You can read through the panels and the character dialogue bubbles.

---

## 4. Your Library
The Library is your personal hub where all your generated content is safely stored.

### Filtering and Searching
1. Click on **Library** in the left-hand navigation sidebar.
2. **Search**: Use the search bar at the top to instantly find a specific project by typing in its title (e.g., "Geography").
3. **Category Tabs**: Click on the interactive tabs (**Videos**, **Comics**, **Quizzes**) to filter your library grid and only show specific types of media.

---

## 5. Settings and Profile
- You can access your account settings and profile from the sidebar. 
- *Note: The VLQ platform is strictly designed around a premium Dark Mode aesthetic to reduce eye strain and keep you focused on the vibrant visuals. Light mode is intentionally disabled.*

---

Enjoy learning at the speed of sight with VLQ!
